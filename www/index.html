    Worked flat as two seamed strips (sole+heel, instep+toe) meeting a ribbed cuff/leg tube — no double-pointed needles or picked-up gusset stitches needed.         Add your own pattern for grading

        Enter the numbers exactly as your original pattern has them. This tool won't rewrite your instructions — it scales your stitch and row counts to a different doll, proportionally, so you can plug the new numbers back into your own pattern.

        For "Scale by": pick a circumference (chest, hip, waist...) for stitch counts around the body, and a length (shoulder to waist/hip/mid-thigh/knee/elbow/wrist) for row counts — total rows, where shaping starts, hem depth, sleeve length. Using a length for a length keeps proportions honest even when two dolls have different torso-to-leg ratios; using height for everything is a rougher stand-in.

        Both come from what you've already saved in Dolls and Gauge & Yarn — add the doll or gauge there first if it's not listed.
